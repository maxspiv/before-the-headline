Investigation import schema (version 1)

A self-contained JSON document describing an investigation: its articles, relevance labels, optional replay timeline and open uncertainties. Download a working example from the template endpoint (GET /api/import/template), then POST it to POST /api/investigations/import with Content-Type: application/json. Request bodies are limited to 1 MiB.

timeline_notesnoOptional context (≤ 10 strings of ≤ 300 chars), date_axis_meaning, empty_interval_meaning.

Errors

All validation problems are collected and returned together: 400 {"errors": ["articles[2].claimed_timestamp.value: ..."]}. Unknown top-level or article fields are rejected as unknown field errors. Imported relevance and duplication labels are treated as supplied, never verified by this app; URLs are stored, never fetched.
