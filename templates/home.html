Methodology Data sources, review method and a separate historical data check

Review method. Each page in an investigation was opened and read during review; relevance and copied-text labels were recorded by hand. The bundled collection mixes GDELT results with pages found outside GDELT.

Timestamps. Article dates on cards and timelines are publisher-claimed dates from the page or from GDELT metadata; date-only entries have no time or timezone. GDELT observation times and capture times are shown separately in source details.

Historical data check. Separately from the investigations above, six translated GDELT GKG files (00:00 and 00:15 UTC on 1 March 2015, 2017 and 2019; 14,725 records) were checked for source-language labels: 60 reported language-code values and 14,320 distinct document identifiers; manual review confirmed 6 non-English article bodies. The equivalent native-stream sample had every language field blank, so an English denominator was not established. No detector, alert threshold, lead time or false-alarm rate was evaluated. Details: HISTORICAL_FEASIBILITY.md in the repository.
